# Questions and answers

## Can I buy groceries in DealSouk?

No. DealSouk only shows prices. You still shop in the store.

## How do I know a price is not old?

Every price shows how old it is. After 72 hours it is marked as old, and the freshest price comes first.

## What stops people from sharing false prices?

A moderator checks each shared price against its photos. A price hides itself after three different people flag it. Moderators can warn, suspend or ban a user, and every action is written to an audit log.

## Is my phone number safe?

It is encrypted in the database. Photos are stripped of their location data and scanned for viruses before anyone sees them.

## What are tokens for?

Approved prices, daily challenges and streaks earn tokens. Tokens turn into supermarket vouchers.

## Why is the moderator website the only one I can open?

The Vercel project called `deal-souk` builds the moderator console. The shopper app needs its own project. See [Deploying](Deploying.md).

## Which languages are supported?

English and Arabic. Arabic flips the whole app to right to left and shows prices with Arabic digits.

## Can I make the text bigger?

Yes. Profile, Settings, Text size: Standard, Large or Extra large. The screens are tested at 160 percent.
